Class Schedule

Welcome, fellow Daoist. Classes run live every week over Zoom. Times below are Sydney time (AEST/AEDT) — use the converter to check your own timezone before booking.

We invite you to join our classes and learn alongside our growing global community. Wherever you are, you can start your journey to better health, wellbeing, and personal and spiritual growth. It is often a challenge to find somewhere to learn genuine Daoist teachings — you don't need to look any further. Our community gathers every week to practise live online over Zoom, across both the free introductory classes and the ongoing groups for those who want to study more seriously.
