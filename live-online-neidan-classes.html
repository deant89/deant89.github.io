Live Online Neidan Classes: WuLiupai Neidan System

Live online Neidan classes in the WuLiupai internal alchemy system, transmitted from the School of Immortals and Buddhas.

The WuLiupai School, also known as ‘The School of Immortals and Buddhas’ was founded by Wu Chongxu and Liu Huayang and the primary goal is to preserve precious Daoist Wisdom and teachings. The WuLiupai Neidan system, also known as the Ancient Northern Method of Wang Chongyang gives the human being the tools to embark on a profound journey of self-cultivation and activation of one’s hidden abilities and potential through the levels of practice called Earth, Human, Heaven.

At the Earth level, which is the initial and most fundamental and important stage upon which all others rest on, we learn how to perform deep cleansing of the body. What is the importance of this? To start the alchemical process, your body and mind must be a clean vessel for further changes and transformations. If we even just focus on the effects of cleansing on everday life, it is absolutely life changing! Everyone knows the importance of hygiene, cleaning your house and keeping it tidy, cleaning your car and you know the feeling of stepping into a clean room compared to a chaotic one. The same process of cleaning up is needed inside the body; this is an amazing secret hidden in plain sight. You will be amazed at how your life improves by applying the principles of cleanliness not just in the external world but even more importantly, in your body, your mind and spirit!

For the first time in history, this method is now widely available to anyone on Earth via Live Online classes! This method was created by Wang Chongyang at the times of Jin dynasty. And represents the unique combination of Ancient Daoism of Laozi and Huangdi and later Daoism with its main concept of purifying the heart. Practicing this method thoroughly you will be able to gradually start to understand the REAL meaning of Dao De Jing as a practical guidance and not philosophical writing.
